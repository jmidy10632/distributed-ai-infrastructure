# Changelog

All material changes to the working paper and collaboration framework will be recorded here.

## 0.1 - 2026-11-09

### Added

- Initial working paper by John D. Midyette.
- Explicit working-draft and political-neutrality statements.
- Explicit geographic-neutrality statement and a global-applicability review question.
- Author's Note in the working paper and an expanded account of the project's origin in the repository.
- Consistent periods after numbered section and subsection identifiers.
- Explicit long-term objective to develop an open standard and open-source reference framework through evidence, working code, testing, and public collaboration.
- Structured questions for collaborators.
- Evidence ledger and project-outcome registry.
- Contribution, governance, conduct, licensing, citation, and security guidance.
- Issue and pull-request templates for evidence-based review.

### Known limitations

- Core sources require independent verification.
- No reproducible proof of concept has been completed.
- Available distributed capacity has not been measured.
- Environmental comparisons have not been validated across representative workloads.
- The trust, attestation, compensation, and operational-governance designs remain proposals.
