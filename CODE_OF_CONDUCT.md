# A Simple Standard for Conversation

This project should be a place where people can disagree without being treated as enemies.

Please address the claim, source, measurement, or design rather than the character or motives of another person. Corrections, counterexamples, and negative results are welcome. So are questions from people who are still learning the subject.

Please do not treat one country, region, language, or infrastructure model as inherently normal or superior. Local knowledge is valuable, and geographic scope should be stated rather than assumed.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing, impersonation, spam, fabricated evidence, deliberate distortion of a source, and partisan campaigning are not welcome.

Strong disagreement is not misconduct. It becomes a problem when it turns into a personal attack, knowingly misrepresents another person's position, exposes private information, or makes productive discussion impossible.

If moderation is necessary, I will try to explain the reason and preserve any useful factual evidence. Sensitive reports should not be posted in public. Once the repository is hosted, use GitHub's private reporting tools when they are available.
