## What the status words mean

- **Verified** means the primary source has been checked independently or through a reproducible extraction.
- **Supported** means the source has been checked, although the interpretation may still need work.
- **Cited** means the source is in the draft but has not yet had an independent check.
- **Disputed** means credible conflicting evidence exists.
- **Withdrawn** means the claim was removed because the support was inadequate or superseded.

## The rule I intend to follow

A source should not receive a better status because it supports the idea, and it should not receive a worse status because it challenges it. Quality, method, relevance, and scope are what matter.
