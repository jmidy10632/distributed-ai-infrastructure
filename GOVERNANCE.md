# How This Project Is Maintained

The purpose of this project is to find out whether the proposed approach holds up. It is not here to protect a conclusion that has already been made.

The work is politically and geographically neutral. No country or region is treated as the normal case against which every other place is measured. Claims should remain within the geographic scope of their evidence.

## Initial stewardship

I am the project's initiator, initial author, and editor. During the working-draft phase, I am also responsible for repository administration, keeping the work focused, reviewing proposed changes, correcting mistakes, and releasing new drafts. This is a temporary stewardship arrangement, not a claim of exclusive authority or an assertion that one person has all of the expertise the project requires.

The project should actively seek critical reviewers, domain specialists, contributors, and prospective maintainers. As qualified and trustworthy contributors demonstrate sustained participation, repository administration, review, and moderation should be shared with them. No major implementation or standards decision should depend solely on one person once a functioning maintainer group exists.

Shared responsibility should be earned through consistent participation, sound judgment, technical care, respectful conduct, and a demonstrated commitment to political neutrality, geographic neutrality, open criticism, and evidence-based decision-making. Familiarity or agreement with the initial author is not a qualification by itself, and disagreement with the paper is not a disqualification.

If the project grows beyond a small working group, its maintainers should document roles, access levels, decision procedures, and a process for adding or removing maintainers. At that point, repository ownership should move to a structure that does not leave the project's continuity dependent on one personal account.

## How decisions will be made

Evidence matters more than popularity. A widely held view can still be wrong, and a difficult counterexample can be more useful than a large number of approving comments.

When a change affects a material claim, I will look at the quality of the source, the limits of the evidence, reasonable alternatives, and whether the claim can be tested. Relevant financial or organizational interests should be disclosed. No one should be the only reviewer of a claim in which that person has a direct stake.

Not every disagreement will end in consensus. When a decision remains uncertain, that uncertainty should be visible rather than smoothed over.

## Criticism and dissent

Good-faith criticism will not be removed because it challenges the premise of the paper. An objection may remain open even when a proposed wording change is not accepted. Future readers should be able to see the strongest unresolved concerns and judge them for themselves.

Ordinary moderation still applies. Harassment, personal attacks, spam, doxxing, fabricated evidence, partisan campaigning, and the disclosure of sensitive information do not become acceptable because they appear in a technical discussion.

## Versions

The 0.x releases are working drafts. Important changes will be recorded in the changelog, and Git history will preserve claims that are later narrowed or removed.

Version 1.0 should wait until the central sources have been checked and the architecture has been tested with at least one bounded, reproducible proof of concept, or until the work clearly documents why such a test is not feasible.

If the project later includes code, datasets, or benchmarks, each one will need its own ownership, licensing, security, and validation information. Experimental work will be described as experimental.
